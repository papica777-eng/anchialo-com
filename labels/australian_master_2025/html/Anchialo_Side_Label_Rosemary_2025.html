TOPICAL GEL WITH BLACK SEA LYE A natural topical gel containing minerals and trace elements derived from Lake Pomorie and Lake Burgas, on the Bulgarian Black Sea coast.
WHAT IS LYE? Black Sea lye is a mineral-rich liquid that forms naturally during the evaporation of seawater in salt lakes along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast. As the water evaporates, salt settles at the bottom, and a dense, dark, non-crystallised liquid called lye forms on top. This lye contains high levels of natural minerals like magnesium sulfate, magnesium chloride, and sodium chloride. Thanks to its rich mineral content and natural alkalinity, Black Sea lye has traditionally been used to support skin health, ease muscle and joint discomfort, and help the body recover after physical activity.
PURPOSE This is a topical body gel formulated to provide the natural benefits of magnesium sulfate and magnesium chloride, absorbed through the skin to help relax and nourish the body. It supports hydration and contributes to a feeling of refreshment and revitalisation. The high magnesium content may assist in maintaining healthy skin and supporting overall well-being. Enhanced with Rosemary extract, traditionally used to support circulation and invigorate the skin.
INGREDIENTS
AQUA (WATER), MINERALS DERIVED FROM BLACK SEA LYE (MAGNESIUM SULFATE, MAGNESIUM CHLORIDE, SODIUM CHLORIDE), HYDROXYETHYLCELLULOSE, GLYCERINE, ROSMARINUS OFFICINALIS LEAF POWDER EXTRACT.
USAGE Apply topically to the desired area and massage thoroughly into the skin. Leave it on for 30 minutes to several hours, then rinse off with water. For best results, leave it on overnight to support deeper relaxation and recovery. Do not apply to open wounds, as the salts may cause stinging or irritation.
WARNING For external use only. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes. This product is not a substitute for medical treatment.
STORAGE Store at 5°C – 25°C / 41.0°F – 77.0°F. Note: Black Sea lye may be stored at higher temperatures, as long as it is not exposed to direct sunlight.
