SACRED BALNEOLOGY HERITAGE

The Miracle of Lake Pomorie

Nestled on a narrow rocky peninsula extending into the Bulgarian Black Sea, Lake Pomorie is a rare hyper-saline lagoon separated from the open sea by a natural sandbar. For millennia, sea water has naturally filtered into shallow salterns under the intense Balkan summer sun.

Through natural fractional solar crystallization, common sodium chloride settles as pure white sea salt, while the remaining super-dense liquid — known in Bulgaria as Luga (medical lye) — concentrates magnesium chloride, sulfate, bromine, iodine, and bioactive peloids.
