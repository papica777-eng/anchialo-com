THE GEOLOGICAL PHENOMENON OF POMORIE LAKE

Balneotherapy & Mineral Science

Formed over millennia along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast, Pomorie hypersaline lye is an ultra-concentrated mineral elixir possessing an unmatched elemental density of over 77 active micro- and macro-elements.

Why Transdermal Dual Magnesium?

Oral magnesium supplements suffer from poor gastrointestinal absorption and laxative side effects. Transdermal application allows ionic Magnesium Chloride (MgCl₂) and Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO₄) to saturate directly into cutaneous and muscular tissues within minutes, restoring intracellular mineral balance without passing through the digestive barrier.
