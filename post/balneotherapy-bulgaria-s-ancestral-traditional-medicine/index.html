Balneotherapy Heritage: From Ancient Anchialos to Pomorie

By Sotir Kichukov (ONE Championship Veteran & Founder of CPT MMA Melbourne) · Lake Pomorie Mineral Archive

From Ancient Anchialos to Modern Pomorie

Located on a narrow Black Sea peninsula 20 km from Burgas, the coastal town of Pomorie carries millennia of maritime history. In the 6th century BC, Greek settlers named the settlement Anchialos — literally meaning 'by the salt sea'. During Roman times and up through the 20th century, travelers visited the lagoon for traditional sea-salt harvesting and mineral bath rituals.

What Balneology Means in Everyday Self-Care

The word balneotherapy comes from the Greek 'balneo' (bath) and describes the age-old cultural tradition of soaking in mineral-rich coastal springs, lagoons, and salt waters to relax, refresh the body, and pamper the skin.

A Tradition Trusted by Bulgarian Combat & Olympic Athletes

Pomorie's coastal resorts, including Grand Hotel Pomorie, have long hosted Bulgarian national wrestling teams, MMA and kickboxing champions, and Olympic competitors for post-camp rest and mineral bath routines. Among those athletes is Sotir Kichukov, ONE Championship veteran and founder of CPT MMA in Melbourne, who now brings authentic Pomorie Lake Dual Magnesium cosmetics directly to Australian athletes and wellness studios.
